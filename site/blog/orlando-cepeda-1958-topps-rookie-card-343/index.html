This is the Baby Bull's rookie card, issued in the Giants' first season in San Francisco, the year he won NL Rookie of the Year. Hall of Fame rookie cards from the '50s are cornerstone vintage pieces.

About Orlando Cepeda

"The Baby Bull" won the 1958 NL Rookie of the Year with the Giants and the 1967 NL MVP with the Cardinals. He hit 379 home runs and was elected to the Hall of Fame in 1999.

About the set

1958 Topps was the first Topps set to feature the new Los Angeles Dodgers and San Francisco Giants after their move west, along with the debut of All-Star subsets.
