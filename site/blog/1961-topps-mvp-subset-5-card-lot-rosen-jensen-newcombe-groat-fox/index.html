This five-card lot from the beloved 1961 MVP subset honors past MVP winners: Al Rosen, Jackie Jensen, Don Newcombe, Dick Groat and Nellie Fox. It's a big step toward completing the subset.

About Al Rosen

Al Rosen won the 1953 AL MVP unanimously for Cleveland, just missing the Triple Crown.

About Don Newcombe

Don Newcombe was the first player ever to win Rookie of the Year (1949), MVP and the Cy Young Award (both 1956). The longtime Dodgers ace was traded to Cincinnati in 1958, which is why he appears in a Reds uniform here.

About Dick Groat

Dick Groat won the 1960 NL MVP and batting title, leading the Pirates to the championship. He was also an All-American basketball player at Duke.

About Nellie Fox

Nellie Fox won the 1959 AL MVP for the pennant-winning "Go-Go" White Sox. One of the hardest hitters ever to strike out, he was elected to the Hall of Fame in 1997.

About the set

1961 Topps is a 587-card set featuring the first cards of the expansion Angels and Senators, a fan-favorite MVP subset honoring past winners, and scarce high numbers (#523-589).
