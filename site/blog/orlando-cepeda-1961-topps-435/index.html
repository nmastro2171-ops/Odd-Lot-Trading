About Orlando Cepeda

"The Baby Bull" won the 1958 NL Rookie of the Year with the Giants and the 1967 NL MVP with the Cardinals. He hit 379 home runs and was elected to the Hall of Fame in 1999.

About the set

1961 Topps is a 587-card set featuring the first cards of the expansion Angels and Senators, a fan-favorite MVP subset honoring past winners, and scarce high numbers (#523-589).
