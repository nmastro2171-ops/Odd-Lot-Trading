Two all-time greats share one card: Willie Mays and Stan Musial. It's one of the most famous combo cards of the '60s.

About Willie Mays

The "Say Hey Kid" hit 660 home runs, won 12 Gold Gloves and two NL MVPs, and made "The Catch" in the 1954 World Series. Many still call him the greatest all-around player who ever lived.

About Stan Musial

"Stan the Man" collected 3,630 hits (split evenly between home and road), won three NL MVPs and seven batting titles, and was elected to the Hall of Fame in 1969.

About the set

1963 Topps featured a round inset photo and bold color bar, plus fun combo cards like "Friendly Foes" and "Pride of the NL." The high numbers (#523-576) are the scarcest series.
