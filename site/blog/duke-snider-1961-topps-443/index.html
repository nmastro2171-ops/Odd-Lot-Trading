Here's the Duke in a Los Angeles Dodgers uniform on his 1961 Topps card. He remained a key bat for the club after its move west. A classic Hall of Fame card at a reasonable price.

About Duke Snider

"The Duke of Flatbush" hit 407 home runs and 11 World Series homers, the most by any National Leaguer. He moved west with the Dodgers and finished his career with the Mets and Giants. He was elected to the Hall of Fame in 1980.

About the set

1961 Topps is a 587-card set featuring the first cards of the expansion Angels and Senators, a fan-favorite MVP subset honoring past winners, and scarce high numbers (#523-589).
