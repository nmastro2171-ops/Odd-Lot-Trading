This is a scarce high-number All-Star card of the manager who had just led Pittsburgh past the Yankees in the 1960 World Series. 1961 Topps high numbers were printed in lower quantities and are some of the toughest cards in the set.

About Danny Murtaugh

Danny Murtaugh managed the Pirates to World Series titles in 1960 and 1971, and as skipper of the defending NL champs he managed the National League All-Star squad.

About the set

1961 Topps is a 587-card set featuring the first cards of the expansion Angels and Senators, a fan-favorite MVP subset honoring past winners, and scarce high numbers (#523-589).
