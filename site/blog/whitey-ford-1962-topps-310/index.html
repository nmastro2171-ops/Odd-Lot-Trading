Whitey Ford's 1962 Topps card follows his 25-4 Cy Young season of 1961, the peak of a Hall of Fame career. The Yankees ace looks right at home inside the classic wood-grain frame.

About Whitey Ford

"The Chairman of the Board" won 236 games for the Yankees with a .690 winning percentage, captured the 1961 Cy Young Award, and holds the record for most World Series wins by a pitcher. He was inducted into Cooperstown in 1974.

About the set

1962 Topps is famous for its wood-grain borders. Those dark borders chip easily, which makes clean examples tough to find today. The set includes All-Star cards, Homer Kings and multi-player rookie cards.
