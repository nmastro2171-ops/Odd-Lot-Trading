Ken Boyer's 1962 card shows him two years before his MVP and championship season of 1964. A Cardinals favorite at a nice price.

About Ken Boyer

Ken Boyer won the 1964 NL MVP and hit a grand slam in Game 4 of that year's World Series, which the Cardinals won. He also won five Gold Gloves at third base.

About the set

1962 Topps is famous for its wood-grain borders. Those dark borders chip easily, which makes clean examples tough to find today. The set includes All-Star cards, Homer Kings and multi-player rookie cards.
