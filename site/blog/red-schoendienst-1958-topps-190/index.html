About Red Schoendienst

Red Schoendienst was a 10-time All-Star second baseman who won a World Series with the Milwaukee Braves in 1957 and later managed the Cardinals to the 1967 title. He entered Cooperstown in 1989.

About the set

1958 Topps was the first Topps set to feature the new Los Angeles Dodgers and San Francisco Giants after their move west, along with the debut of All-Star subsets.
