About Joe Medwick

"Ducky" Medwick won the 1937 NL Triple Crown and MVP, the last National Leaguer to win a Triple Crown. He was inducted into the Hall of Fame in 1968.

About the set

1960 Fleer Baseball Greats is a 79-card set of retired legends from the game's early decades, printed in bright pastel colors. For many Hall of Famers of the 1900s-1930s it is one of the only affordable vintage cardboard options.
