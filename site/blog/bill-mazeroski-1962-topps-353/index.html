About Bill Mazeroski

Bill Mazeroski hit the only Game 7 walk-off home run in World Series history to beat the Yankees in 1960. A slick-fielding second baseman with eight Gold Gloves, he was elected to the Hall of Fame in 2001.

About the set

1962 Topps is famous for its wood-grain borders. Those dark borders chip easily, which makes clean examples tough to find today. The set includes All-Star cards, Homer Kings and multi-player rookie cards.
