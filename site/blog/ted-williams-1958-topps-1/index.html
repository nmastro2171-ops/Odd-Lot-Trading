Ted Williams is card #1 in the 1958 Topps set. Number-one cards are prized because they sat on top of rubber-banded stacks and took the most wear, so nice ones are tough. This is a true vintage centerpiece.

About Ted Williams

"The Splendid Splinter" was the last man to hit .400 (.406 in 1941), won two Triple Crowns and hit 521 home runs despite losing nearly five seasons to military service. He was elected to the Hall of Fame in 1966.

About the set

1958 Topps was the first Topps set to feature the new Los Angeles Dodgers and San Francisco Giants after their move west, along with the debut of All-Star subsets.
