This seven-card lot of all-time greats on Allen & Ginter's retro design includes Babe Ruth, Mickey Mantle, Hank Aaron and Ken Griffey Jr. It's a great starter lot for a young collector or a gift.

About Babe Ruth

Babe Ruth hit 714 home runs, won seven World Series titles and remade the game. He was one of the five original Hall of Fame inductees in 1936.

About Hank Aaron

Hank Aaron hit 755 home runs, still holds the career RBI record and made more All-Star teams than any other player. He was elected to the Hall of Fame in 1982.

About Ken Griffey Jr.

"The Kid" hit 630 home runs, won 10 Gold Gloves and the 1997 AL MVP, and owned one of the prettiest swings the game has ever seen. He entered Cooperstown in 2016 with 99.3% of the vote, a record at the time.

About the set

Allen & Ginter mixes baseball greats with oddball subjects on a retro tobacco-card design inspired by the original 1880s Allen & Ginter cards.
