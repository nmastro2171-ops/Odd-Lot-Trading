This combo card unites two Brooklyn Dodgers legends, Duke Snider and Gil Hodges. Both were Hall of Famers and longtime teammates.

About Duke Snider

"The Duke of Flatbush" hit 407 home runs and 11 World Series homers, the most by any National Leaguer. He moved west with the Dodgers and finished his career with the Mets and Giants. He was elected to the Hall of Fame in 1980.

About Gil Hodges

Gil Hodges was an eight-time All-Star first baseman for the Dodgers who later managed the 1969 "Miracle Mets" to the title. He was elected to the Hall of Fame in 2022.

About the set

1963 Topps featured a round inset photo and bold color bar, plus fun combo cards like "Friendly Foes" and "Pride of the NL." The high numbers (#523-576) are the scarcest series.
