Big Klu appears on 1954 Topps, the year he led the NL in home runs and RBIs. It's a great early-'50s card.

About Ted Kluszewski

"Big Klu" led the NL with 49 homers and 141 RBIs in 1954 and was famous for cutting the sleeves off his jersey to free his massive arms.

About the set

1954 Topps is a classic two-image design with a large portrait beside a smaller action shot, and it holds key rookies of Hank Aaron, Ernie Banks and Al Kaline.
