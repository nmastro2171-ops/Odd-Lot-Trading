This 1954 Bowman card shows Eddie Mathews in his early Milwaukee years, right after his 47-homer breakout in 1953. It's a terrific vintage Hall of Fame card from the Bowman-Topps rivalry era.

About Eddie Mathews

Eddie Mathews hit 512 home runs and is the only player to suit up for the Braves in Boston, Milwaukee and Atlanta. The Hall of Fame third baseman won the 1957 World Series with Hank Aaron.

About the set

1954 Bowman went head to head with Topps during the hobby's great bubble-gum card war, with bright color portraits framed by a clean white border and a facsimile autograph on the front.
