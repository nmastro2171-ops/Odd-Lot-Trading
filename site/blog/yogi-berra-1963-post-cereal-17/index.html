About Yogi Berra

Yogi Berra won 10 World Series rings as a player, the most in history, along with three AL MVP awards and 18 All-Star selections. He was inducted into Cooperstown in 1972.

About the set

Post Cereal printed baseball cards right on the back of its cereal boxes in the early '60s. Kids cut them out by hand, so each one is unique, and nicely trimmed examples are prized.
