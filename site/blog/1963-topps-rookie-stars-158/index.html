Multi-player Rookie Stars cards were Topps' way of introducing a crop of young players at once. They're an inexpensive way to own a genuine 1963 card and an important part of completing the set.

About the set

1963 Topps featured a round inset photo and bold color bar, plus fun combo cards like "Friendly Foes" and "Pride of the NL." The high numbers (#523-576) are the scarcest series.
