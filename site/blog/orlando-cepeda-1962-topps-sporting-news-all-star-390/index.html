Here's Orlando Cepeda on a 1962 All-Star card, earned with his monster 1961 season when he led the NL in homers and RBIs. A great vintage Giants card.

About Orlando Cepeda

"The Baby Bull" won the 1958 NL Rookie of the Year with the Giants and the 1967 NL MVP with the Cardinals. He hit 379 home runs and was elected to the Hall of Fame in 1999.

About the set

1962 Topps is famous for its wood-grain borders. Those dark borders chip easily, which makes clean examples tough to find today. The set includes All-Star cards, Homer Kings and multi-player rookie cards.
