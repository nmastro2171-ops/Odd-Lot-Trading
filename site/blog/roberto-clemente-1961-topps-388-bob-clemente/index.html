Early in his career, Topps called him "Bob" Clemente, and this 1961 card is one of the issues that carries that name. It shows him coming off the Pirates' 1960 championship and on the verge of his first batting title. Vintage Clemente cards are always in demand.

About Roberto Clemente

Roberto Clemente collected exactly 3,000 hits, won 12 Gold Gloves, four batting titles and the 1966 NL MVP. He died in a 1972 plane crash while delivering relief supplies to Nicaragua and was elected to the Hall of Fame in a special election the following year.

About the set

1961 Topps is a 587-card set featuring the first cards of the expansion Angels and Senators, a fan-favorite MVP subset honoring past winners, and scarce high numbers (#523-589).
