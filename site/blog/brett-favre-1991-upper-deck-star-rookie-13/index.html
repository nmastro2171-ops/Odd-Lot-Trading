About Brett Favre

Brett Favre won three straight NFL MVPs and Super Bowl XXXI with the Packers, but he was drafted by the Atlanta Falcons in 1991, which is why his true rookie cards show him in Falcons red. He was inducted into the Hall of Fame in 2016.

About the set

Cards from the late '80s and early '90s were printed in huge numbers, but sealed packs from the era are a nostalgic time capsule and a fun, low-cost rip.
